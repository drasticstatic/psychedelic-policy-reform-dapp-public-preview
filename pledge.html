Questions

Why a wallet signature?

A signature shows that a pledge came from a particular wallet without asking for a name, an email or anything else about you. It’s free, and it can’t move any funds.

Is anything stored?

No. The signature is checked against your connected wallet inside this browser tab and then forgotten. This page doesn’t show or keep your wallet’s address, and it never sends anything.

What happens later?

Recording pledges comes only after the evidence library is underway, and only with a clear choice about what’s recorded. The roadmap says where it fits, and the changelog will say when it opens.

Do I need a wallet?

Only to try the signature. Everything else here, the evidence and the updates, is open to everyone.

Not legal advice. This is general information about the law and public sources, written for understanding. It can’t account for anyone’s situation. For advice, talk to a lawyer licensed where you live.
