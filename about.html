About

About the initiative

Psychedelic Policy Reform is a policy and evidence initiative built on the Religious Freedom Restoration Act. It’s seen from Pennsylvania, and it’s meant to serve communities whose sincere spiritual practice includes sacraments the law still restricts.

Not legal advice. This is general information about the law and public sources, written for understanding. It can’t account for anyone’s situation. For advice, talk to a lawyer licensed where you live.

What it does

  • Gathers evidence: public sources on the law, court decisions, research and practice, each one cited and checked.
  • Explains it plainly, in words anyone can follow, starting with RFRA in plain words (a draft, to be reviewed).
  • Keeps an open record of every step in its AstroLog.

It’s a policy and evidence lane. It doesn’t tell anyone’s personal story, and it can’t give legal advice.

Where it started

The spark was Kevin M’s “Roadmap to Reform” masterclass: a program on changing psychedelic laws through real-world policy work, from the advocate behind Decriminalize Denver, the 2019 campaign that made Denver the first US city to decriminalize psilocybin mushrooms. It inspired Christopher W to bring this work home to Pennsylvania.

The communities at its heart

Four life-changing healing communities are at its heart: Psychedelics In Recovery™ (PIR®), Psanctuary, ORG (the Octagon Religious-Research Group) and The Holy Earth Foundation. This initiative wants to see their work protected, and blessing Pennsylvania. The roadmap describes each one in its own public words, and what this work can offer it.

Naming a community, or the masterclass that inspired this work, describes who this work hopes to help and where it came from. It doesn’t mean any of them has endorsed the initiative or taken a position on it, and nothing here speaks for any of them. PIR®, as a 12-step fellowship, keeps to its own traditions.

Where people gather

For now, the conversation happens in Facebook groups in Pennsylvania: two that Christopher W manages, Pennsylvania Psychedelic Reform and Pennsylvania RFRA, and two related NORML groups. They’re all in the Community window.
